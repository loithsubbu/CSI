🚨 The 18% GST Hidden Trap

Merchant "No-Cost EMI" schemes are mathematically not 0%. While the merchant provides an upfront discount equal to the bank interest, the RBI mandates that banks collect 18% GST on each monthly interest payment.

Hidden Cost: You pay ₹1,381 in non-refundable GST that is never refunded even if you return the item!
💰 Opportunity Cost & Time Value

When paying upfront cash, you forfeit the yield that cash could have earned if kept in fixed deposits or liquid mutual funds (compounding at 7.0% p.a.).

Forgone Yield: Keeping ₹1,00,000 in debt saves cash liquidity, but bank interest (12.0%) far exceeds FD yield (7.0%), making debt mathematically disadvantageous.
⚠️ Default & Penal Compounding Risk

Missing even a single EMI payment triggers severe compound consequences: flat late fee penalties plus 18% GST, and overdue balances incur compounded penal interest at 24% - 36% p.a.
